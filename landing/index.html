How it works Open the app

Most of the business comes from customers who order again.

The engine reads the weekly sales export and finds three groups: first-time buyers who are due a thank-you or a nudge, regulars who have gone quiet, and Palazzo orders that need a fit check before cutting. Staff get one ranked list and suggested messages. Nothing is sent automatically, and no customer data is stored.

What the engine does

Reorder nudges

First-time buyers hear from you at day 30, 60 and 90: a thank-you, a new print that matches, then a small perk.

Win-back

Regulars who have gone quiet for about seven months are listed by past spend, with a personal message to send.

Fit check

Palazzo pants are cancelled more often than other styles. Confirm hip size and leg width before cutting.

Did it work?

Compare customers you messaged with the held-back group, so you know the messages, not luck, brought people back.

Staff access

The Retention Engine is for Immaculate Vibes 254 staff. Ask the owner for the password. The app may take up to a minute to wake if nobody has used it recently.
